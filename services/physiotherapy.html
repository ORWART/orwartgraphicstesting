Rehabilitation at Home

Our physiotherapy services bring professional rehabilitation to your home. We help clients recover mobility, strength, and function after surgery, injury, or illness with personalized exercise programs.

Services Include:

  • Post-surgical rehabilitation programs
  • Mobility and gait training
  • Strength and conditioning exercises
  • Balance and fall prevention training
  • Pain management techniques
  • Stroke and neurological rehabilitation
  • Home exercise program design
  • Assistive device training

Why Home Physiotherapy?

  • Convenient: no travel to clinic needed
  • Functional training in real home environment
  • Family can learn to assist with exercises
  • Licensed physiotherapists with hospital experience
