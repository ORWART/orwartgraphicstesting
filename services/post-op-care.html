Supporting Recovery at Home

Our post-operative care services ensure safe, comfortable recovery at home with professional monitoring and support tailored to your specific surgical procedure and needs.

Services Include:

  • Nursing observation and assessment
  • Wound care and dressing changes
  • Medication management and pain control support
  • Personal care and hygiene assistance
  • Mobility assistance and fall prevention
  • Nutrition and hydration support
  • Health education for self-care
  • Monitoring for complications
  • Communication with family and healthcare team

Why Choose Our Post-op Care?

  • Reduces risk of hospital readmission
  • Personalized recovery plans per surgeon's protocol
  • 24/7 on-call nursing support available
  • Coordination with physiotherapy for rehabilitation
