Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services

Lenorplus provides comprehensive home healthcare services including bedside nursing, caregiving, elderly care, post-operative care, palliative care, physiotherapy, mental health support, family support services, orthopedic care, and child mental health services. We serve clients in Kampala and surrounding areas.

You can request care by filling out our online form, calling us at 0752 406556 or 0763 609851, or emailing bedsidelenorplus@gmail.com. Our care coordinator will contact you within 24 hours to discuss your needs and schedule an assessment.

We primarily serve Kampala and the surrounding metropolitan area, including Mutungo, Biina, Muyenga, Kansanga, Bukoto, Naguru, Kololo, Nakasero, Bugolobi, and nearby communities. Please contact us to confirm if we can serve your specific location.

Costs vary based on the type of service, duration, frequency, and level of care required. We provide transparent pricing after an initial assessment. Contact us for a personalized quote — there's no obligation for an initial consultation.

We work with several insurance providers. Coverage depends on your specific policy and plan. Please contact us with your insurance details, and we'll help verify your benefits and explain any out-of-pocket costs.

We do our best to match clients with caregivers based on skills, personality, and schedule compatibility. While we cannot guarantee a specific caregiver, we prioritize consistency and will work with you to find the best fit. If you're not satisfied, we'll arrange a replacement.

For urgent care needs, call our main line at 0752 406556. We have staff available for emergency assessments and can often initiate services within 24 hours. For medical emergencies, please call 112 or 999 immediately.
