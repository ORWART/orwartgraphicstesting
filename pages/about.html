Who We Are

Lenorplus Home Health and Bedside Care is a professional home-based healthcare and supportive-care service provider serving individuals, families and healthcare partners in Kampala, Uganda.

We provide professional care services that support clients requiring nursing care, caregiving assistance, rehabilitation, mental-health support, palliative care, post-operative care and other home-based services.

Our Services Extend To:

  • Recovering from illness or surgery
  • Managing chronic health conditions
  • Aging with dignity and support
  • Experiencing challenges associated with aging
  • Receiving palliative support
  • Transitioning from hospital to home
